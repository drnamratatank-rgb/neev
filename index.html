What is Neev?

Healthcare rarely happens through one doctor, one hospital or one service.

There are consultations, reports, medications, investigations, specialists, recovery, home support and family decisions — often spread across different people and providers. Neev helps bring these pieces together: we take in the situation, identify what is needed, bring in the appropriate support and stay involved as needs change.

We are not another healthcare provider added to the list. We help the healthcare around you work better together.

Navigate Care

When health changes, the medical problem is only one part of the picture.

A hospital discharge may bring new responsibilities home. A diagnosis may involve several specialists. Recovery may require nursing, rehabilitation, equipment and family coordination. Neev helps bring these moving parts together.

The objective is not to add more care. It is to provide the right care, at the right time, with the right people involved.

For Families

When one person's health affects the whole family.

Healthcare decisions often involve much more than the person receiving treatment — work commitments, distance, ageing parents, children, travel, multiple opinions and the everyday responsibility of making things happen. Neev acts as a steady point of contact for these families: someone who reads the full picture, brings the right support together, and keeps everyone in the loop.

Especially when:

  • Parents live independently
  • Family members live in different cities or countries
  • Health needs are changing
  • Care has become difficult to coordinate
  • A family wants to be better prepared before a crisis

Care for the individual. Clarity for the family.

Your family may be in India. You don't have to manage everything from afar.

The Neev Standard

What we hold ourselves to, on every case.

A relationship built on trust needs a standard behind it, not just a promise. This is how we run every engagement, regardless of size.

One named point of contact

Not a rotating helpline — someone who knows the case and stays with it.

Verified, briefed caregivers

Every nurse, caregiver or attendant is background-checked and clinically briefed before entering a home.

A documented care plan

Shared with the family in writing, not held only in someone's head.

Scheduled clinical review

A clinician checks in at agreed intervals — not only when something goes wrong.
